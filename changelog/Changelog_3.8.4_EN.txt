3.8.4
- Item guides for every hero. Edit and export custom guides, or import them at startup.
- Bots for 31 heroes. The host and judges can add them with `-bots` before round one.
- Fixed quick-casts and a shop hotkey desync. Hotkeys are suspended while typing in chat or search. Added bindings for the three side backpack slots.
- Full time stops freeze cooldowns and pierce counterstances against targets without Time Resistant or Time Manipulator.
- Time Resistant lets heroes act during time stops with −50% MS/AS. Added to Jiren and Sabrac, and replaces Time Manipulator for Shana and Time Sphere owners.
