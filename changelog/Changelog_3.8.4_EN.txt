3.8.4
- Item guides for every hero in the shop. Edit, save and export custom guides, or import them at startup.
- Bots for 31 heroes. The host and judges can use `-bots` before round one. Bots shop, use abilities and items, and respond to emotes and pings.
- Fixed several quick-casts and added bindings for the three side backpack slots.
- The shop hotkey opens the window locally. Hotkeys are suspended while typing in chat or search.
- Full time stops freeze cooldowns and pierce counterstances if the target has neither Time Resistant nor Time Manipulator.
- Time Resistant allows actions during time stops with −50% MS/AS and frozen cooldowns. Added to Jiren and Sabrac, and replaces Time Manipulator for Shana and Time Sphere owners.

**Heroes**
**Dead Master**
- At 5/10/15/20 souls, permanently unlocks a 1.5-sec F root, +150 W range, a third Q skull and +100 R radius, respectively.
- D. Souls from fallen heroes unlock at level 20. From level 1, every 7,000 energy earned grants a soul without consuming energy.
- Q. Damage halved. Cooldown is 18 sec. from the last throw, with up to 5 sec. between throws. A skull recovers in 8 sec. and lasts 4 sec., or 8 sec. with a soul. With Ctrl+E, two empowered skulls within 700 form a wall.
- W. Damage reduced by 1× INT. With Ctrl+E, knocks enemies back and stuns for 1 sec.
- E. From level 20, Ctrl+E toggles spending one soul per cast to enhance Q/W/F/R/G.
- F. Casting on an enemy spends two skulls and stuns for 0.1 sec. each second.
- R. Damage reduced by 1× INT. Added an 80% MS/AS slow.
- G. Drains HP/MP and grants energy. Transfers 15% of the enemy's max HP, with excess forming a 12-sec shield.

**Uryu Ishida**
- Q. Can move with a 50% MS penalty. Normal attacks are disabled and F preserves the barrage. Arrow damage reduced by 10%. Break distance is 1400.
- T. Removes the Q movement penalty and raises break distance to 1700. Normal attack hits blink Ishida around the target.

**Gokudera Hayato**
- W. Rain-Storm Shot range increased from 1500 to 1600.

**Kurotsuchi Mayuri**
- E. Dash and stab for 2–4× INT and mark for 7 sec. The target's next cast triggers a 2-sec silence and increases control duration and percentage strength by 25% for 5 sec. Passive poison damage halved. Cooldown 28–20 sec.
- R. Ctrl+R selects the bomb effect.
- T. Press T again to set the poison cloud's center. MS/AS slow reduced from 70% to 40%.

**Sasagawa Ryohei**
- T. Fixed charge visibility.

**Haku**
- Starting AGI 25 → 23, growth 3.2 → 3.0.
- Q. Fires 12 pairs of senbon. Ctrl+Q switches between firing at a point and turning to fire forward.
- W. Ice Strike moved to W. Mana 80–180.
- E. Ice Senbon Rain can be learned from level 6. Damage 3–7× AGI, root 2 sec., mana 125–225.
- F. Right-click to travel between mirrors. Levels 6/12/18 allow 1/2/3 new mirrors. Entering an existing mirror preserves its remaining lifetime and costs no charge.
- F. Flight damage 1.5× AGI, radius 150, range 500. Press F again to dash 200 out. Mirror HP is 2× total AGI. Breaking the occupied mirror stuns Haku for 0.5 sec. The panel shows charges and cooldown. Mana 50, recharge 15 sec. per mirror.
- R. Damage 5–9× → 4–8× AGI.
- G. Shunshin no Jutsu moved to G. Duration 2 sec., cooldown 35–15 sec.
- T. Enemies cannot walk out of the circle.

**Hit**
- D. Up to 10 charges from ability hits on heroes. Each grants +4 AGI and +0.1 sec. of time stop. Active cooldown reduction is capped at 5 sec.
- T. The sequence and time stop last 6 sec. including the retreat and pose.
