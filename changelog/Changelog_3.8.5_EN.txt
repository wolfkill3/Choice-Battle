3.8.5
- Added Homura Akemi, Iori Yagami and Might Guy.
- Added a Nanaya Shiki bot with combat combos and its own item build.

**Heroes**
**Hit**
T
- Cast time 0.35 sec. A 0.2-sec windup adds a stance, blue aura and voice line before the dash.

**Homura Akemi**
- New INT hero with base and Devil forms.
Q
- Five variants: shield, double pistol shot, bazooka, sniper rifle and flash grenade. The weapon also changes basic attacks. Pistol/bazooka/rifle range 1500/2500/4000. Shield and pistol cooldown 14/13/12/11/10 sec.
- The rifle bullet grants 650 vision and cancels the target’s current order on hit. The bazooka knocks enemies away in the direction of the shot.
W
- Fires a ten-round burst. Moving or issuing another order interrupts the burst.
E
- Lobs a rocket that stuns for 1 sec. throughout the blast area. Flight time scales with distance.
R
- Homura blinks around the chosen area and throws six grenades. Area radius 550, grenade blast radius 350. Enemies can move out of the area.
D
- Blinks up to 750, changes weapon and resets Q cooldown. Ctrl+D selects the next weapon or random mode. Self-casting changes weapons in place with a 2-sec cooldown.
F
- From level 12, records a living ally’s position within 1200 for 6 sec. Recast to return them from any distance. Ctrl+F switches between manual and automatic recall.
G
- From level 25, bonds with an ally. Saving them from lethal damage restores 30% HP and unlocks an optional transformation until round end. Madoka is selected automatically and recovers 45% HP. Madoka becoming a Goddess also unlocks the form.
T
- Stops time across the map for 4–5 sec. Projectiles and supported sustained attacks resume when time returns. D cooldown during the stop is 1.5 sec. From level 35, the chosen ally can also act.
**Devil Homura**
- Dark energy basic attacks at 900 range. G displays a passive form icon. Bonus mana recovery is 40 MP/sec.
Q
- Area damage of 7 × INT and a 2-sec root. Supports point quick-cast.
W
- The curse gains stacks when the target uses abilities. Stacks increase damage over time, slows and their ability cooldowns.
E
- Grants an ally +40 to all attributes, a shield worth 7 × Homura’s INT, and time-stop immunity for 20 sec.
R
- A 3-sec. area deals 10 × INT total across six hits. AS/MS slow builds from 20% to 60%, and magic resistance falls by 25%.
D / F / T
- D blinks up to 1200. Recast within 3 sec. to return. F selects an ally anywhere on the map. T stops time for 5 sec.

**Iori Yagami**
- New AGI hero with purple flames and an alternate skin. His abilities burn enemies and heal Iori when they hit.
- Burn deals 1.5 × AGI per application and skips ticks against invulnerable targets.
R
- A charged projectile creates a line of fire pillars and stuns enemies hit. Cooldown 35 sec.
F / G
- Follow up on other techniques. Cost 40/55/70/85/100 MP.

**Jellal Fernandes**
G
- Flame of Rebuke unlocks at level 25. Grants +20 to all attributes for 60 sec. Can target Jellal, allies or enemies.

**Might Guy**
- New hero in the Naruto tavern. E opens the Eight Gates in sequence, increasing attributes and changing F, G and D. At level 35, E starts at the sixth gate.
Q / T
- Both abilities have a follow-up. Q adds a leaping strike, and T unlocks a stronger second blow.
R
- Lifts enemies while spinning. The finisher stuns and knocks targets back.
D / F / G
- Gates unlock Omote Renge, Ura Renge, Asakujaku, Hirudora, Night Guy, Sekizo and Air Step.

**Orihime**
G
- Rokuten Shishun unlocks at level 25. After a 2-sec windup, Orihime dashes 2500. She and the first enemy true hero hit are paused and invulnerable for 1 sec. The seal bursts for magic damage equal to 100% of Orihime’s max HP through shields and Boros Armor, then Orihime dies. Cooldown 50 sec.

**Ruby**
- The Kaleidostick Ruby projectile freezes during a time stop and resumes flight afterwards.
